# OziClock Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 26, 2026**

OziClock is developed by OziOs. This policy explains how the OziClock desktop application handles data.

## Summary

OziClock does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information for advertising, analytics, or tracking. It does not include telemetry or advertising SDKs.

## Data stored on your device

OziClock stores its settings and the information you create in the application locally on your device. This can include clock configurations, appearance settings, alarms, timers, reminders, tasks, calendar and planner entries, and local diagnostic information used to investigate application failures.

OziClock uses this data only to provide its features. The developer does not receive this data.

## Optional folder sync

OziClock can optionally synchronize selected data with a folder that you choose. For example, you may select a folder managed by a desktop synchronization service such as OneDrive, Google Drive, or Dropbox.

This feature is off by default. OziClock does not request or store your credentials for those services. When you enable sync and choose a folder, OziClock writes the data you selected to that folder and may read it back to synchronize your devices. The privacy practices of the service that manages that folder are governed by that service's own privacy policy.

## Notifications and external links

OziClock may display local operating-system notifications for alarms, timers, reminders, tasks, or calendar events. Notification content is generated from data stored on your device.

The application can open the OziClock GitHub repository in your web browser when you choose to use a related link. OziClock does not send browsing history to the developer.

## Data security and retention

Your locally stored data remains on your device until you change or delete it, uninstall the application, or remove the relevant application data. Any data you choose to synchronize remains in the folder and service you selected until you remove it there.

## Children

OziClock is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the [OziClock GitHub repository](https://github.com/ozinka/OziClock/issues).
